- nxapi 1.6.1-next.257 — AGPL-3.0-or-later. Source: https://github.com/samuelthomas2774/nxapi . Its license is included by npm in the installed package. The unmodified published CLI entry point handles authentication and collection using its own configuration. A local preload adapter sets the SplatNet read-request language to zh-CN; client registration and token handling are not replaced.
